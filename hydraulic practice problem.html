A Venturi meter in a 300-mm main has a coefficient of discharge of 0.98. The piezometric head difference between the inlet and the throat is 4.5 m of water. Three interchangeable throats are available: 150 mm, 175 mm and 200 mm.

ITEM 1Fundamentals of Fluid Flowmedium

Determine the discharge with the 150-mm throat.

SolutiondD = 0.5 and 1 − (d/D)4 = 0.9375.

The 150-mm throat is the smallest of the three, so it passes the least.
ITEM 2Fundamentals of Fluid Flowmedium

A wider throat lowers the throat velocity but raises the area — here the area wins.
